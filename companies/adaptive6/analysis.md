## Interview Questions

### A. Founders & Leadership (5 questions)

1. **"Tell me about a time the three of you strongly disagreed on strategy. How did you resolve it?"**
   - *Why:* Tests founder dynamics under pressure. Conflicts emerge at growth stage.

2. **"What's the biggest mistake you've made as a founding team in the first 2 years?"**
   - *Why:* Red flag if they can't name one. Shows self-awareness.

3. **"How do you make decisions when incentives diverge?"** (e.g., growth vs. profitability)
   - *Why:* Real founders face trade-offs. Vague answer = problem.

4. **"If AWS aggressively bundled your features natively, how would you respond?"**
   - *Why:* Tests strategic thinking about existential threat.

5. **"What would cause you to step down from your current role?"**
   - *Why:* Signals if founders are committed or looking for exit.

### B. Product-Market Fit (5 questions)

1. **"Who are your top 3 customers by revenue? Can I speak with them?"**
   - *Why:* Concrete validation. If they say no, that's a red flag.

2. **"What's your net revenue retention? How many customers have churned and why?"**
   - *Why:* Cloud FinOps should have HIGH retention (sticky products). Low NRR = weak moat.

3. **"What's your median enterprise sales cycle? How many deals in pipeline?"**
   - *Why:* Understand go-to-market efficiency.

4. **"How much are your customers actually saving? Do you have ROI proof?"**
   - *Why:* Do the "billions" claim translate to real customer outcomes?

5. **"What percentage of Fortune 500 have you penetrated? What's your target?"**
   - *Why:* Market share reality check. If <0.5%, still searching for fit.

### C. Competitive Moat (4 questions)

1. **"How is your AI model fundamentally different from AWS/Azure/GCP native tools?"**
   - *Why:* THE critical question. If they can't answer clearly, there's no moat.
   - Expected answer: Speed of optimization? Accuracy? Automation? Cross-cloud?
   - Bad answer: "Ours is better AI" (vague)

2. **"What happens if AWS bakes this into Cost Explorer Pro and charges $100/month?"**
   - *Why:* Tests realistic threat modeling.

3. **"What's preventing a customer from switching to native cloud tools?"**
   - *Why:* Stickiness = switching cost + lock-in + unique value. What's yours?

4. **"How defensible is your position in 3 years? What's your long-term strategy?"**
   - *Why:* Vision beyond immediate survival.

### D. Burn Rate & Runway (4 questions)

1. **"What's your monthly burn rate and how much runway do you have?"**
   - *Why:* At 30 people + $44M, runway should be 18-24 months. Less = risky.
   - Math: $44M / (burn/month) = runway months

2. **"What revenue do you need to hit to reach cash-flow positive?"**
   - *Why:* Understand profitability path.

3. **"What's your growth target for next 18 months? Revenue? Customers? Team size?"**
   - *Why:* Understand if they'll hit breakeven or need Series B.

4. **"What happens if you miss growth targets? What's your contingency?"**
   - *Why:* Stress test the plan.

### E. Team & Culture (5 questions)

1. **"How has the team composition changed since Series A? Who left and why?"**
   - *Why:* Early attrition signals problems. Track record matters.

2. **"What's your biggest talent gap right now? Why hasn't it been filled?"**
   - *Why:* Shows what they value. Unfilled gaps = resource constraint.

3. **"How do you maintain culture as you scale from 30 to 100 people?"**
   - *Why:* Israeli startups often have tight founding cultures. Scaling is hard.

4. **"What's the biggest frustration people have with working here?"**
   - *Why:* Honest answer reveals real culture state.

5. **"How do Israeli founders think about global expansion / US market?"**
   - *Why:* Enterprise is mostly in US/EU. Is there a plan?

### F. Role-Specific (3-4 questions)

1. **"What does success in this role look like in 6 months vs. 18 months?"**
   - *Why:* Vague answer = bad planning. Specific answer = clear vision.

2. **"What's the biggest bottleneck this hire solves?"**
   - *Why:* Should be specific, not generic.
   - Good answer: "We're losing deals to Nuvemshare on analytics — we need a world-class data scientist"
   - Bad answer: "We need to grow the team"

3. **"Who will I report to and what's their track record?"**
   - *Why:* Manager quality = team quality.

4. **"What's the biggest risk to this role succeeding in the next 18 months?"**
   - *Why:* Honest answer shows strategic thinking.
